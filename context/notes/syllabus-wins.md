# Syllabus of record

The four files in `context/curriculums/` are the teachable units, the order, and the outcomes. Cloned repos and `toc/` outlines are seeds for examples and for checking that a unit is not an odd invention.

When a clone orders a topic differently, follow the curriculum `After` fields. When a clone uses a different name for the same idea (partitioning vs sharding, message queue vs work queue), use the curriculum slug in the app and mention the other name once in the guide.

This app does not teach LeetCode problem writeups. `../leetcode-coach` owns problem practice, company lists, and mini games. Guides here may name a classic prompt ("two sum", "number of islands") as the situation that makes the concept show up. They do not paste a problem statement.
